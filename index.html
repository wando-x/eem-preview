How it works

Four simple steps

  1. 01

    Request

    Send the property address, the package you want, and any notes through the contact form.

  2. 02

    Schedule

    We agree on a time. Shoots are scheduled on weekends, Saturdays and Sundays during daytime hours.

  3. 03

    Shoot

    We capture the property from the air, weather and site conditions permitting.

  4. 04

    Delivery

    Edited files are delivered within 24 hours of the flight.

Every flight is subject to weather, location, and site conditions, and whether a flight takes place is always at the pilot's discretion. If a flight can't happen, we'll work with you on another option.
